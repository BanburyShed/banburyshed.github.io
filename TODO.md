• The Restart Project – Campaign promoting the "Right to Repair", advocating for stronger rules that enable consumers to repair products rather than replace them. Provides educational resources and practical support for repairing electronic devices. 1
• Right to Repair UK Campaign – Awareness and advocacy initiative explaining why binding repair rights are needed, including access to spare parts, repair information and longer product lifecycles. 1
• DIY Device Repair Programme – Collection of self-help resources encouraging individuals to repair devices themselves using guides, troubleshooting tips and repair documentation. 1
• Repair Guide Networks – Promotion of resources such as repair wikis and online repair guides that help people fix cameras, laptops, printers, smartphones and household appliances. 1
• Community Repair Events – Encouragement to participate in local repair-focused community events where volunteers help diagnose and repair items. 1
• Restart Parties – Community-led events where people bring broken electronics and receive support to repair them, extending product life and reducing waste. 1
• Repair Café Movement – Volunteer-led repair cafés that help repair electrical goods, clothing, bicycles and household items rather than disposing of them. Featured as practical examples of circular economy activity. 12
• Waste and Sustainable Consumption Week – Sustainability Network campaign focused on changing behaviours around waste, consumption, reuse and resource efficiency across NESO. 3
• "See Waste Differently" Campaign – Educational strand encouraging colleagues to think differently about waste and the lifecycle of everyday objects through the "Social Life of Rubbish" theme. 3
• Upcycling / Repurposing Challenge – Challenge encouraging employees to transform discarded items into something useful and give materials a second life. Also linked to creating new internal waste labels and signage. 34
• Repurposing Awareness Campaign – Communications campaign explaining how value can be rediscovered through reuse, repair, adaptation and creative repurposing of items, ideas and resources. 5
• Rubbish Storytelling Competition – Engagement campaign inviting colleagues to tell the story of an object's transformation and changing value, highlighting reuse and repurposing themes. 34
• Repair Café and Recycling Charity Spotlight Programme – Series of Sustainability Network communications showcasing local repair cafés, recycling charities and organisations that extend the life of products and materials. 32
• Refill and Zero-Waste Shopping Promotion – Campaign highlighting refill shops, bulk-buy stores and low-waste retail options that reduce packaging waste and encourage sustainable consumption. 2
• Library of Things / Borrow Don't Buy Initiative – Promotion of schemes allowing people to borrow tools and equipment instead of purchasing new items, reducing consumption and waste. 2
• Furniture Reuse and Reclaimed Materials Promotion – Showcasing organisations focused on refurbished, upcycled and reclaimed furniture as alternatives to purchasing new products. 2
• Food Waste Reduction Initiatives – Promotion of services and programmes aimed at reducing food waste through surplus food redistribution and reusable delivery systems. 2
• Carbon Footprint Reduction Sprint – Sustainability challenge encouraging employees to reduce waste associated with energy use, travel and food consumption through small behavioural changes. 34
