In this week's Shednanigans, Graham turned Harry Potter wands for his daughter, Jim (and Smithy!) crafted wood spoon MkII, whilst Dave constructed a radio transmitter, Wynn worked on the Wood Chipper, and Bob began to re-create the Rocking Horse.

Sadly, the Wood Chipper turned out to be beyond repair, although the project as a whole was a great exercise in sustainability; keeping appliances from landfill, bringing people together, and sharing skills.
