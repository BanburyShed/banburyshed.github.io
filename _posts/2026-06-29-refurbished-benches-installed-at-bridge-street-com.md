Very pleased that we have now installed the refurbished benches in Bridge Street Garden Banbury in conjunction with [Banbury Community Action Group](https://banburycag.org.uk).
The refurbishment project was supported by [UK Mens Sheds Association](https://menssheds.org.uk/)  as part of their Shoulder to Shoulder project which is a nationwide initiative, delivered in partnership with [Diageo’s responsible drinking platform DRINKiQ](https://www.drinkiq.com/en-gb/ukmsa/shoulder-to-shoulder), inviting Sheds to build buddy benches for their local communities.
These benches are more than just somewhere to sit — they’re conversation starters. A place to pause, connect, and quietly support wellbeing beyond the Shed walls.
