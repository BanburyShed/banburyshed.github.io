A lovely piece of our twinning history has found a new home!
Chairman of the Twinning Association, John Donaldson officially presented a historic stained glass panel to Banbury Mayor Cllr Mark Cherry. 
Originally gifted to the Banbury & District Twinning Association by Karl Kreuzberg over 20 years ago, this stunning piece was rescued from the old town hall in Allner and depicts iconic landmarks from our German twin town of Hennef. 
A huge thank you to the talented volunteers at Banbury Shed, who built a bespoke, illuminated wooden box to showcase it in all its glory! 
We were delighted to be joined by town councillors, association members, and Council Leader Cllr Shaida Hussain, who kindly thanked our team for our ongoing work celebrating our international ties.
