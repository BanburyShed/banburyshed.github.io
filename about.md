## About Banbury Shed {#about-heading}

Banbury Shed is a welcoming community workshop where people can share practical skills,
collaborate on projects, and enjoy good company. We are part of the Men's Shed movement —
open to everyone who wants to learn, make, and connect.

Although we are part of the Men's Shed movement we are deliberately not exclusive to men — all are welcome, but for insurance reasons you have to be an adult.

Whether you are interested in woodworking, electronics, upcycling, or simply want
a cup of tea and a chat, you are welcome here. No experience is necessary — just
curiosity and enthusiasm.

We meet nearly every Saturday morning at 09:30 at People's Park, Banbury — check the notice at the top of this page for our next meeting date.
New members are always welcome, just turn up!

<img src="/assets/images/banbury-town-crest.jpg" alt="Banbury Town Crest" width="240"/>

We operate from the Long Shed in People's Park in Banbury thanks to the
generosity of [Banbury Town Council](https://banbury.gov.uk). We are very
grateful for their kind support.

Banbury Shed &amp; RVS Community (our official full name) is a branch of the
[Royal Voluntary Service](https://www.royalvoluntaryservice.org.uk). Branch
number
[47369](https://www.royalvoluntaryservice.org.uk/our-services/search/oxfordshire-hub/banbury-shed-and-rvs-community-47369000/).

## Frequently Asked Questions {#faq-heading}

### What tools do you have?

We have lots of tools and equipment for woodworking, metalworking and electronics projects. These include:

- Woodworking lathes
- Pillar drill
- Small metalworking lathe
- Hand tools (hammers, screwdrivers, saws, chisels, files etc.)
- Fasteners: screws, nuts, bolts, rivets etc.
- Bandsaws
- Chop saws

### Can you teach me how to use the tools?

Of course! We are a sharing environment. Also, if you have any skills, we'd love to learn from you.

### Can I bring children along?

Unfortunately due to insurance reasons we only admit adults into the shed.

### Are there any tools you don't have?

For insurance reasons we are not able to use:

- Table saws
- Welding equipment
- Anything involving a naked flame or fire

### How much does it cost?

Most of our full time members pay £10 per month via standing order to our bank account.
Alternatively you can pay £5 per session.
These subs are entirely optional and on the basis of your ability to pay.
