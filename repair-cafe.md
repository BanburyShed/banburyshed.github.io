<section aria-labelledby="repair-cafe-heading">
  <h2 id="repair-cafe-heading">What is a Repair Caf&eacute;?</h2>
  <p>
    A Repair Caf&eacute; is a free community event where volunteers with practical skills help
    people repair broken items - rather than throwing them away. Clothes, furniture,
    electrical appliances, bicycles, crockery, and more can all find a second life at
    a Repair Caf&eacute;.
  </p>
  <p>
    The idea started in Amsterdam in 2009 and has since spread to thousands of locations
    around the world. It saves money, reduces waste, and brings people together.
  </p>
</section>
