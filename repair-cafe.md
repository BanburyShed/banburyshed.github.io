## What is a Repair Café? {#repair-cafe-heading}

A Repair Café is a free community event where volunteers with practical skills
help people repair broken items — rather than throwing them away. Furniture,
electrical appliances, bicycles, crockery, and more can all find a second life
at a Repair Café.

The idea started in Amsterdam in 2009 and has since spread to thousands of
locations around the world. It saves money, reduces waste, and brings people
together. Plus, repairing things yourself is rewarding!

## Repair Café at Banbury Shed {#our-repair-cafe-heading}

Banbury Shed runs drop-in Repair Café sessions every Saturday. Bring along
something you'd like to try fixing and our volunteers will do their best to
help. Tea and coffee are always on!

Keep an eye on our [Schedule](/schedule/) page to see the dates of our upcoming
sessions.
